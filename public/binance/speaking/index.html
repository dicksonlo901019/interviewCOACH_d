高機率面試題High-probability interview questions

01

請做一分鐘自我介紹

Please introduce yourself in one minute.

中文版

我有約十年產品管理經驗，近幾年專注在交易所、資產帳戶與風險控制。最相關的一段經歷，是我帶領三位產品經理和兩位設計師，與跨區團隊在四個月內完成一個交易所平台從零到一上線。我負責產品架構、帳戶規則、需求文件與跨團隊協作；工程與資料團隊負責技術實作。這個職位需要處理資產、損益、對帳與錢包體驗，和我的經驗很接近。我希望進一步了解團隊目前最需要解決的問題。

ENGLISH VERSION

I have around ten years of product management experience, with a recent focus on exchanges, asset accounts, and risk controls. In my most relevant role, I led three product managers and two designers and worked with a cross-regional team to launch a new exchange platform in four months. I owned the product structure, account rules, requirements, and cross-functional alignment. Engineering and data teams owned the technical implementation. This role is closely related to my experience in assets, P&L, reconciliation, and wallet journeys. I would like to understand the team’s most important problems today.

02

為什麼對幣安和這個職位有興趣？

Why are you interested in Binance and this role?

中文版

我有興趣，不只是因為品牌，而是工作內容和我的經驗直接相關。我做過交易所帳戶架構、資產快照、估值、充提流程與對帳需求。這個職位要把多種帳戶和資料整理成使用者看得懂、信得過的結果。產品規模更大，複雜度也更高，這正是我想繼續深化的方向。

ENGLISH VERSION

I am interested not only because of the brand, but because the work is directly related to my experience. I have worked on exchange account structures, asset snapshots, valuation, deposit and withdrawal flows, and reconciliation requirements. This role needs to turn complex account data into results that users can understand and trust. The larger scale and complexity are exactly what I want to deepen next.

03

為什麼離開上一份工作？

Why did you leave your previous role?

中文版

我離開上一份工作的原因是組織調整與人力縮編，不是主動離職。那段經歷讓我累積了數位資產錢包、對帳需求與產品協作流程的實務經驗。現在我希望下一個角色能更直接使用我在交易所帳戶、資產資料與交易產品上的核心能力。

ENGLISH VERSION

I left my previous role because of an organizational adjustment and headcount reduction. It was not a voluntary resignation. That role gave me practical experience in digital-asset wallets, reconciliation requirements, and product delivery workflows. I am now looking for a role where I can apply my core experience in exchange accounts, asset data, and trading products more directly.

04

你是否接受合約職？

Are you open to a contract role?

中文版

我願意認真評估，因為工作內容和我的經驗很接近。在做決定前，我希望先確認合約期限、續約條件、雇用主、福利，以及這個職位的發展方式。只要安排清楚，而且雙方對成果有一致期待，我願意繼續討論。

ENGLISH VERSION

I am open to considering it seriously because the scope is closely related to my experience. Before making a decision, I would like to confirm the contract length, renewal criteria, employing entity, benefits, and development path. If the setup is clear and both sides agree on the expected outcomes, I am happy to continue the discussion.

05

你認為自己最大的經驗缺口是什麼？

What is your biggest experience gap?

中文版

我做過交易所帳戶、資產快照、估值與對帳需求，但沒有負責過幣安規模的資料平台，也沒有親自實作完整的成本計算引擎。我的強項是先把範圍、資料來源、例外與驗收方式定清楚，再和工程及資料負責人完成技術方案。大規模系統的實際架構，是我進入團隊後需要快速理解的部分。我不會把相鄰經驗說成已經做過。

ENGLISH VERSION

I have worked on exchange accounts, asset snapshots, valuation, and reconciliation requirements, but I have not owned a data platform at Binance scale or implemented a complete cost-basis engine. My strength is defining the scope, data sources, edge cases, and acceptance criteria clearly, then aligning with engineering and data owners on the technical solution. The actual large-scale architecture is something I would need to learn quickly after joining. I would not present adjacent experience as direct ownership.

06

你會怎麼處理損益與成本計算？

How would you approach P&L and cost-basis calculation?

中文版

我會先把定義說清楚，再談資料和畫面。第一步是確認哪些帳戶與資產要計算。第二步是定義買入、賣出、轉帳、手續費和價格來源怎麼處理。第三步是準備範例和例外情境，讓產品、資料與工程用同一套規則驗收。畫面也要告訴使用者計算範圍、更新時間與明細來源。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would clarify the definition before discussing data or screens. First, I would confirm which accounts and assets are included. Second, I would define how buys, sells, transfers, fees, and price sources are handled. Third, I would prepare examples and edge cases so product, data, and engineering can validate the same rules. The screen should also explain the calculation scope, update time, and source details.

07

你會怎麼優化整個錢包體驗？

How would you improve the full wallet experience?

中文版

我不會一開始就做視覺改版。我會先列出主要頁面和使用流程，再按照問題的傷害程度排序。錯誤的餘額或狀態最優先，其次是被卡住的資金操作，再來才是理解困難和視覺一致性。每個流程都要處理載入中、等待中、失敗和受限制等狀態。成效要同時看任務完成率、錯誤、客服量與資料品質。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would not start with a visual redesign. I would first list the main pages and user journeys, then prioritize problems by harm. Wrong balances or statuses come first, followed by blocked money movement, then confusion and visual consistency. Every journey must cover loading, pending, failed, and restricted states. Success should combine task completion, errors, support demand, and data quality.

08

你會怎麼開始做全量對帳？

How would you start full reconciliation?

中文版

我會先確認每條業務線要比對什麼、誰負責，以及什麼情況才算一致。接著統一資料識別、時間、狀態與基本平衡規則。差異要分成缺資料、重複、延遲、沖正和人工調整，並指定負責人與處理時限。我會先選一個高風險但邊界清楚的範圍跑完流程，再逐步擴大。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would first confirm what each business line reconciles, who owns it, and what counts as a match. Next, I would align identifiers, time, states, and the basic balance rule. Differences should be classified as missing, duplicate, late, reversed, or manually adjusted, with a clear owner and response time. I would complete one high-risk but well-bounded area first, then expand step by step.

09

你會怎麼優化充值與提現？

How would you improve deposits and withdrawals?

中文版

重點不是少一個步驟，而是避免不可逆的錯誤。我會優先處理幣種與網路選擇、地址驗證、可用餘額、費用、限額和安全檢查。當交易等待中或進入人工審查時，使用者要知道現在發生什麼、還需要做什麼，以及何時可以求助。實際規則要和風險、法遵與工程負責人確認。

ENGLISH VERSION

The goal is not simply to remove a step. It is to prevent irreversible mistakes. I would prioritize asset and network selection, address validation, available balance, fees, limits, and security checks. When a transaction is pending or under manual review, users should know what happened, what they need to do, and when they can ask for help. The exact rules must be confirmed with risk, compliance, and engineering owners.

10

你怎麼和風險及法遵團隊合作？

How do you work with risk and compliance teams?

中文版

產品經理不應自己解釋或放寬法規。我會先確認政策來源、版本、適用對象、觸發條件與決策負責人，再把它轉成可測試的產品狀態。使用者要知道下一步，但不能看到可能被規避的控制細節。營運端也需要原因代碼、審查權限、處理時限與完整紀錄。政策決定仍由風險、法遵與法務負責。

ENGLISH VERSION

A product manager should not interpret or weaken regulation independently. I would confirm the policy source, version, affected users, triggers, and decision owner, then translate it into testable product states. Users need a clear next step without seeing control details that could be bypassed. Operations also needs reason codes, review permissions, response times, and complete records. Risk, compliance, and legal teams remain the policy owners.

11

你會怎麼設計帳戶總覽？

How would you design an account overview?

中文版

第一步是讓使用者快速回答三個問題：我有多少資產、資產在哪裡、數字何時更新。我要先定義哪些帳戶會納入總額，再區分可用、鎖定、等待中和負債。不同資產要用一致的價格來源與時間換算。內部轉帳不能造成重複計算。資料不完整時，畫面要明確顯示，而不是假裝數字仍然即時。

ENGLISH VERSION

The first goal is to help users answer three questions quickly: how much they have, where the assets are, and when the numbers were updated. I would define which accounts are included in the total, then separate available, locked, pending, and liability balances. Different assets need a consistent price source and timestamp. Internal transfers must not create double counting. If data is incomplete, the screen should say so instead of presenting an old number as current.

12

使用者看到的數字不一致，你會怎麼處理？

How would you handle a data discrepancy seen by users?

中文版

我會先確認影響範圍，並避免使用者繼續依賴錯誤數字。接著比對資料來源、識別碼、時間、狀態和計算版本，找出差異從哪一段開始。短期要有清楚的使用者說明與處理入口，長期要補上監控、對帳規則與責任人。修正後如果需要重算，也要清楚標示數字已更新。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would first confirm the impact and stop users from relying on an incorrect number. Then I would compare the data sources, identifiers, timestamps, states, and calculation versions to find where the difference begins. In the short term, users need a clear explanation and a support path. In the long term, the team needs monitoring, reconciliation rules, and ownership. If the result must be recalculated, the correction should be clearly identified.

13

跨團隊意見不同時，你怎麼推進？

How do you move forward when teams disagree?

中文版

我會先把爭議寫成明確的決策題，而不是讓大家一直討論立場。接著列出使用者影響、風險、資料證據、限制與可選方案。需要專業判斷的部分，由對應負責人決定；產品經理負責把選項和代價整理清楚。如果仍無法決定，我會找有權責的人裁決，並把決策和後續行動留下紀錄。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would turn the disagreement into a clear decision question instead of letting teams repeat their positions. Then I would list the user impact, risks, evidence, constraints, and available options. The relevant owner makes decisions that require specialist authority, while the product manager makes the options and trade-offs clear. If the issue remains unresolved, I would escalate it to the accountable decision maker and record the decision and next actions.

14

你的領導方式有什麼改變？

How has your leadership approach changed?

中文版

我以前比較容易直接補上團隊缺少的內容。後來我發現，這會讓品質依賴個人。現在我會先定義文件結構、需求完成條件、驗收方式與負責人，再用評審機制提早找出問題。這樣不是增加流程，而是減少後期反覆修改，也讓團隊更容易獨立交付。

ENGLISH VERSION

Earlier in my career, I often filled in missing work myself. I later realized that this made quality depend too much on one person. Now I define the document structure, readiness criteria, acceptance method, and ownership first, then use reviews to find issues early. The goal is not to add process. It is to reduce late rework and help the team deliver independently.

15

遇到不知道的內部資訊，你會怎麼回答？

How would you answer when you do not know an internal fact?

中文版

我會先直接說明目前不知道，不會把公開資訊當成內部事實。接著我會說明需要向誰確認、要看哪些資料，以及確認後會做什麼決策。如果時間很急，我會清楚標示暫時假設和風險。這樣能讓團隊繼續前進，也不會製造假的確定性。

ENGLISH VERSION

I would state clearly that I do not know yet and would not present public information as an internal fact. Then I would explain who needs to confirm it, what evidence is required, and which decision depends on the answer. If time is limited, I would label the temporary assumption and its risk. This keeps the team moving without creating false certainty.
